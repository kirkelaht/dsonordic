How DSO Nordic works

A controlled disinfection cycle at the farm entrance.

1

Activation

The cycle starts via photo sensor or remote. Sound signal and yellow beacon indicate active operation.

2

Delay

After activation, spraying starts in 5–7 seconds. The vehicle must not enter the gate during this time.

3

Passage

The vehicle passes slowly (approx. 2.4 km/h). Disinfectant is applied from four directions — above, below and sides. A handheld sprayer is included for wheel arches.

4

Cycle duration

The cycle runs up to 60 seconds, or until the second photo sensor / manual stop. Spray rate 100 L/min.

Why vehicle biosecurity matters

Vehicles are one of the main ways pathogens reach a farm site. The European farm sector faces threats including African swine fever and foot-and-mouth disease.

Consequences of infection for the farm

  • Production halt
  • Mass culling of animals
  • Regional isolation
  • Serious economic impact

DSO Nordic helps manage this risk with controlled disinfection at the farm entrance.

Safety and operation

Vehicle requirements

Maximum height 4 m. Minimum ground clearance 120 mm. Close doors, windows and other openings before passage.

Chemical

Designed for approximately 1% solution based on chloramine-T. Consult the manufacturer before using other chemicals.

Safety features

Emergency stop, tank level sensor, sound signal and beacon. The driver must remain in the cab during the cycle.

Installation

Installation area at least 7 × 3 m. Base: asphalt, concrete or compacted fill. Power: CEE 16A, 3-phase. Installation by authorised personnel only.

Frequently asked questions

Short answers to the main pre-sale questions.

Does DSO Nordic work automatically?

Yes. Depending on configuration, the cycle can be activated automatically using sensors or by remote control.

What power connection is required?

CEE 16A three-phase power supply.

How much space is required?

The installation area must be at least 7 × 3 metres.

What is the maximum vehicle height?

4 metres.

Can DSO Nordic be installed on an existing farm entrance?

Yes, provided the installation surface and required space meet the installation requirements.

How often does the system require inspection?

A documented inspection is required at least annually.

What is the delivery time?

Typical delivery time is 6–8 weeks.

Does the system comply with EU machinery requirements?

Yes. DSO Nordic has an EU Declaration of Conformity covering the applicable machinery and EMC requirements.
